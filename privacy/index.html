Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-01

RitualShot (bundle com.ritualshot.app) is published by Rohit Grover / Atul Das. This policy explains how the app handles your information.

Data You Enter

Logs, notes, schedules and reminders you create are stored locally on your device. We do not operate servers that receive this data.

Sync

If the app offers sync and you enable it, your data may be stored in your own private iCloud account via Apple services, governed by Apple's privacy policy. We cannot access it.

Notifications

Reminders use local notifications scheduled on your device. You can disable them in iOS Settings.

Purchases

Optional Pro is purchased through Apple In‑App Purchase. Subscription status is managed by RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous app user ID and purchase receipts, not your log contents. We never receive your payment card details.

No Tracking, No Sale

We do not sell or share your personal data, do not track you across apps or websites, and do not use advertising.

Children

RitualShot is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Deletion

Delete entries in the app, or delete the app to remove local data. Remove iCloud data via iOS Settings.

Health Data Notice

Information you log may be sensitive. It stays on your device or your iCloud; we do not process it.

Changes

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version.
